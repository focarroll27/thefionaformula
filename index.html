The Fiona Formula

The Fiona Formula is a science-driven decision engine that reduces complexity in health, beauty, and lifestyle by translating toxicology, exposure science, and real-world tradeoffs into fewer, better choices.

Unlike product databases, The Fiona Formula evaluates both long-term health impact and real-world efficacy — so recommendations are safe enough to trust and effective enough to use.

The Fiona Formula does not give you ten options — it gives you the default choice that matters most. Additional options only exist when biology meaningfully differs — such as sensitive skin, pregnancy, or early childhood.
