The Fiona Formula

The Fiona Formula is a science-driven decision engine that reduces complexity in personal care products by translating formulation safety, via toxicology, exposure science, and real-world tradeoffs, into fewer, better choices.

Unlike product databases, The Fiona Formula evaluates both long-term health impact and real-world efficacy, so evaluations are comprehensive enough to trust and products are good enough to actually use.

The Fiona Formula gives you a single default choice when it matters most in our effort to eradicate decision fatigue. Additional options only exist when biology meaningfully differs, such as for sensitive skin, pregnancy, or babies.
